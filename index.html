Ivan Stegic has spent his career in the space where people meet technology.

He grew up under the apartheid regime in South Africa, which taught him very early on who gets left out of systems, and why. He earned degrees in both Physics and Psychology, then found himself at Honeywell Technology Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Later, long hours and burnout at a software startup pushed him to choose time with his family and to build something of his own. TEN7 was born!

TEN7 works with mission-driven organizations on websites that carry real public trust. And now that the web has a second audience, it's become more important than ever to build for humans and bots. Ivan created the Readiness Index, an open standard that measures how well machines can find, read, trust and use websites. He wants to be able to quantify how much delight a website can elicit in a human, a work in progress.

Ivan lives in Minneapolis with his two long-haired sausage dogs. He's an eternal optimist and a relentless ambassador for technology, science and common sense.
